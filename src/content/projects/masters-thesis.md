## Thesis Abstract

In this thesis we analysed the genome of <i class='taxon'>Streptomyces cyanogenus</i> S136 in context of the specialized metabolism potential. In particular we annotated the biosynthetic gene clusters using different software, such as deepBGC, antismash and gecco. Made two programs: rRNADif and BGCViz, with Python, R and bash scripts.
